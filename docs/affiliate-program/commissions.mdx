<H2 id="ladder">The ladder</H2>

| Tier         | Rate      | How you reach it                     |
|--------------|-----------|--------------------------------------|
| Bronze       | 20%       | Default for everyone                 |
| Silver       | 25%       | 10+ active monthly traders referred  |
| Gold         | 30%       | 50+ active monthly traders referred  |
| Platinum     | 35%       | 150+ active monthly traders referred |
| Beast Mode   | 45%       | Invite-only, owner-assigned          |
| KOL / custom | up to 50% | Custom rate, time-boxed              |

<Para>
  Earn it, keep it. Once you reach a tier, you stay there. Tiers only ever go
  up. Tiers are re-evaluated hourly. Beast Mode is invite-only and assigned
  at owner discretion, not earned through volume. KOL and high-volume rates
  are custom deals, always time-boxed.
</Para>

<H2 id="active">What counts as an active monthly trader</H2>
<Para>
  A referred user counts as active in a given calendar month if they execute
  at least one fee generating trade through DeepBot during that month. One
  trade in the month is enough. The user counts in your active tally for that
  month.
</Para>
<Para>
  The active tally is recomputed continuously. Tier evaluation runs hourly.
  When your active count crosses a tier threshold, the new rate takes effect
  on the next fee generating trade by any of your referrals.
</Para>

<H2 id="lock">Per trade rate lock</H2>
<Para>
  Commission on a given trade is paid out at your current tier rate at the
  time the underlying trade fee was collected. Once a fee is settled, the
  rate on that fee is locked. Future tier moves apply to future trades.
</Para>

<H2 id="lifetime">Lifetime, not seasonal</H2>
<Pull>
  Your referral is yours for as long as they trade.
</Pull>

<Para>
  No expiry. No 30 day window. No 12 month cap. A user who signed up through
  your link in month one is still your referral in year three, and every
  trade they place is still earning you commission at your current tier rate.
</Para>

<H2 id="custom">KOL and custom rates</H2>
<Para>
  KOL and high volume custom deals run up to <Lime>50%</Lime>. These are
  bespoke arrangements, always time boxed, and assigned at platform
  discretion. Public tiers are earned through volume. Custom rates are not.
</Para>

<Callout kind="info" title="Hold $DEEPAI? Both stack">
  Affiliate commissions are paid from platform fees. The $DEEPAI revenue
  share is paid from a separate pool. Holding $DEEPAI and running referrals
  earns you both. They are independent.
</Callout>
