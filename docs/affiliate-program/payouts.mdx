<H2 id="how">How payouts work</H2>
<Para>
  Affiliate commissions accrue continuously as your referrals trade. Pending
  balance updates in the dashboard in near real time. On the program payout
  schedule, the pending balance settles to your designated Solana wallet as
  USDC.
</Para>
<Para>
  There is no claim window to miss. Earnings do not expire. If your wallet
  is set and the network is up, your payout lands.
</Para>

<H2 id="independent">Independent of $DEEPAI revenue share</H2>
<Para>
  Affiliate commissions are paid from platform fees and settle as USDC on
  Solana. The $DEEPAI revenue share is a separate pool with its own
  eligibility and claim flow. Holding $DEEPAI does not change your affiliate
  payout, and earning affiliate commission does not change your revenue
  share.
</Para>

<Callout kind="info" title="Wallet not set? Balance still accrues">
  Commission keeps accumulating even if you haven't set a payout wallet. The
  pending balance is visible in the dashboard. When you set the wallet, the
  next scheduled settlement clears the balance to it.
</Callout>
