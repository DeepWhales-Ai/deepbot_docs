<H2 id="how">How does the DeepBot affiliate program work?</H2>
<Para>
  Every DeepBot user has a referral link, with no application to fill in and no
  minimum holding to qualify. Share it, and anyone who signs up through it is tied to
  you for life. Every time one of your referrals trades through DeepBot, you earn a cut
  of the platform fee on that trade. The fee itself is 1% on the EVM chains and 1.5% on
  Solana, and your commission is a share of it rather than an extra charge to the
  person trading. Commission runs from a <Lime>20%</Lime> base rate up to{' '}
  <Lime>35%</Lime> across five public tiers, and tiers never go down once reached.
  Earnings accrue continuously and settle in USDC on Solana to a wallet you choose.
  There is no claim window to miss and nothing to sign for. Holding $DEEPAI is not
  required, because affiliate commission is separate from the token revenue share.
</Para>
<Para>
  Commissions are paid in USDC on Solana, settled to a wallet you choose.
  There is no claim window to miss. Earnings accrue continuously and pay out
  on the program schedule.
</Para>

<H2 id="ladder">The ladder</H2>
<Para>
  Five public tiers plus an invite tier and custom rates. The base rate is{' '}
  <Lime>20%</Lime>. The top public tier is <Lime>35%</Lime>. Beast Mode is
  the invite tier at <Lime>45%</Lime>. KOL and high volume custom deals run up to{' '}
  <Lime>50%</Lime>.
</Para>
<Para>
  Tiers are earned by referring active monthly traders. Once you reach a
  tier, you stay there. Tiers only ever go up.
</Para>
