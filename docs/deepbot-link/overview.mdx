<H2 id="what">What is DeepBot LINK?</H2>
<Para>
  <DeepBot /> LINK is the web surface of the same platform Dale runs in Telegram,
  live now at <a href="https://trade.deepbot.pro" className="db-link" target="_blank" rel="noopener"><Lime>trade.deepbot.pro</Lime></a>. It is a progressive web app you sign
  into with Telegram, so the wallet, holdings and history you see are the ones you
  already have. Once you are signed in you get native charts, the convergence signal
  feed, a holdings view with a dust filter and wallet labels, and a Swap Box for
  placing trades. Nothing needs to be recreated and nothing is copied across from a
  separate account. The one thing that genuinely differs from Telegram is
  authorization. Web trading is authorized by your session. There is no PIN prompt on
  the web surface, no spend gate and no second authorization step before a trade
  executes. The Telegram PIN surface is separate and unchanged. Treat the two as
  different security models rather than one product in two windows.
</Para>

<H2 id="pin">Is there a PIN on the web?</H2>
<Para>
  No. Web trading is authorized by your session and nothing else. A trade placed
  through the Swap Box executes without a PIN prompt, without a spend limit and
  without asking you to authorize again. This is a deliberate design decision, not a
  missing feature and not a gap waiting to be closed.
</Para>
<Callout kind="info" title="The two surfaces are not equivalent">
  Telegram gates trades behind a PIN. The web does not. Anyone with access to a signed
  in browser session can trade. Sign out when you are finished on a shared or
  untrusted device.
</Callout>
