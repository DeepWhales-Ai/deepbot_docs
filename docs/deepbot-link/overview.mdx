<H2 id="what">What is DeepBot LINK?</H2>
<Para>
  <DeepBot /> LINK is the web surface of the same platform Dale runs in Telegram,
  building now at <Lime>trade.deepbot.pro</Lime>. It is a progressive web app you
  will sign into with Telegram, so the wallet, holdings and history are the ones you
  already have rather than a separate account. It brings native charts, the
  convergence signal feed, a holdings view with a dust filter and wallet labels, and a
  Swap Box for placing trades. One thing is worth knowing before it lands, because it
  genuinely differs from Telegram: web trading will be authorized by your session.
  There is no PIN prompt on the web surface, no spend gate and no second authorization
  step before a trade executes. The Telegram PIN surface is separate and unchanged.
  They are different security models rather than one product in two windows.
</Para>

<H2 id="pin">Will there be a PIN on the web?</H2>
<Para>
  No. Web trading will be authorized by your session and nothing else. A trade placed
  through the Swap Box executes without a PIN prompt, without a spend limit and
  without asking you to authorize again. This is a deliberate design decision, not a
  missing feature and not a gap waiting to be closed.
</Para>
<Callout kind="info" title="The two surfaces are not equivalent">
  Telegram gates trades behind a PIN. The web does not. Anyone with access to a signed
  in browser session can trade. Sign out when you are finished on a shared or
  untrusted device.
</Callout>
