<H2 id="what">Can you trade prediction markets through Dale?</H2>
<Para>
  Yes. Dale connects to <Lime>AGG.market</Lime>, a third party aggregator that routes
  across several prediction venues, and the full round trip runs in conversation. You can
  browse what is trading, take a position, hold it, and close it for cash without leaving
  the chat. Tell Dale a category and Dale answers from live venue data. Say how much you
  want and on which side, and Dale places it. Markets cover politics, sports, crypto
  outcomes and world events.
</Para>

<H2 id="custody">Who holds the funds</H2>
<Para>
  <Lime>Funds are held by the aggregator, not in your DeepBot wallet.</Lime> This is a
  different arrangement from the rest of your trading. When you fund a prediction market
  position, the money leaves the DeepBot wallet and sits with the aggregator for the life
  of the position. DeepBot is not the venue and not a counterparty to your position.
  DeepBot routes the instruction and shows you the result.
</Para>

<H2 id="venues">Which venues you reach</H2>
<Para>
  The aggregator routes across <Lime>Polymarket, Predict, Myriad, Limitless and

  open to one country may be closed to another, and being in a supported country does not
  mean every venue is offered to you.
</Para>

<H2 id="how">How do you take a position?</H2>
<Para>
  Tell Dale a category and Dale returns markets that are trading in it. Dale shows the
  market with its current odds and liquidity. Tell Dale which side you want and how much,
  and Dale shows you what the position costs to open before anything is placed. You
  confirm, and the position opens. Closing works the same way: tell Dale to close it and
  the proceeds settle back through the aggregator.
</Para>

<Callout kind="info" title="Search by market name is not supported">
  You cannot ask for a specific market by its name. Tell Dale a category, such as politics
  or sports, and Dale returns what is trading in it.
</Callout>

<H2 id="resolution">How markets resolve</H2>
<Para>
  <Lime>The venue resolves the market, not DeepBot.</Lime> Each venue runs its own
  resolution process and decides the outcome. A resolution cannot be appealed through
  DeepBot. If you disagree with how a market resolved, that is a matter for the venue that
  resolved it.
</Para>

<H2 id="cost">What does it cost?</H2>
<Para>
  Dale shows the cost to open a position before you confirm it. The prediction venues
  charge their own fees on top of the market price, and those are set by the venue rather
  than by DeepBot. Check the venue for a full fee breakdown if you need the exact split on
  a given market.
</Para>
