<H2 id="what">Can you trade prediction markets through Dale?</H2>
<Para>
  Yes. Dale connects to <Lime>AGG.market</Lime>, an aggregator that routes across
  Polymarket and Kalshi, and the full round trip runs in conversation. You can browse
  what is trading, take a position, hold it, and close it for cash without leaving the
  chat. Ask what the odds are on an event and Dale answers from live venue data. Say
  how much you want and on which side, and Dale places it. Positions sit alongside the
  rest of your holdings and are funded from the DeepBot wallet you already use, so
  there is no separate account to open and no separate balance to top up. Markets
  cover politics, sports, crypto outcomes and world events. Kalshi is a regulated
  venue and will route you through identity verification when a market requires it.
  Polymarket does not ask for that. Access is geo fenced, so which markets you are
  offered depends on where you are.
</Para>

<H2 id="how">How do you take a position?</H2>
<Para>
  Ask Dale what is trading on a topic, or name the event directly. Dale returns the
  market with its current odds and liquidity. Tell Dale which side you want and how
  much, and Dale shows you what the position costs to open before anything is placed.
  You confirm, and the position opens. Closing works the same way: tell Dale to close
  it and the proceeds settle back to your wallet.
</Para>

<H2 id="cost">What does it cost?</H2>
<Para>
  Dale shows the cost to open a position before you confirm it. The prediction venues
  charge their own fees on top of the market price, and those are set by the venue
  rather than by DeepBot. Check the venue for a full fee breakdown if you need the
  exact split on a given market.
</Para>
