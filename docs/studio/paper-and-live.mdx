<H2 id="paper">Paper mode</H2>
<Para>
  <strong>Paper mode</strong> records every trade a hunt would make, at the real market
  price, with no money moving. Use it to see how a hunt behaves before trusting it with
  funds.
</Para>

<H2 id="live">Live mode (PRO)</H2>
<Para>
  <strong>Live mode (PRO)</strong> buys for real from your <DeepBot /> wallet, on the
  hunt's chain, using the buy size you set. Positions carry automatic stop loss and take
  profit.
</Para>
<Para>
  Live hunts use the same trade safety gate as Dale. Before any live buy, the token is
  checked for common traps, and anything that fails is skipped.
</Para>

<H2 id="solana">Solana and Token 2022</H2>
<Para>
  On Solana, hunts handle both standard tokens and Token 2022 tokens. Token 2022 tokens
  with risky settings (such as transfer fees or an active freeze authority) are always
  skipped.
</Para>
