<H2 id="paper">Paper testing</H2>
<Para>
  Paper trading uses simulated money at live prices. Your hunt follows the same rules as a
  live hunt, but nothing real moves. Paper uses a fixed trade size per chain and can't show
  real slippage, fees or a failed swap, so real results can differ.
</Para>
<KeyValueGrid items={[
  { k: "Paper page", v: "Pick a hunt to see its paper state, trade size, open positions and closed trades with their result." },
  { k: "Results page (PRO)", v: "Day, Week, Month or All, a win rate ring, return on closed trades and a By hunt table." },
  { k: "Close by hand", v: "On the Paper page, press Close at current price, then Yes, close." },
  { k: "Reset paper stats", v: "Start your paper totals fresh. Your trades stay, and real results don't change." },
  { k: "Keep on paper", v: "Holds a hunt on paper. Release removes the hold." },
]} />
<Para>
  Only closed trades count towards your win rate and P&L.
</Para>

<H2 id="live">Going live with real money (PRO)</H2>
<Para>
  Live hunts buy on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base and Solana, up to your plan's live hunt limit
  per chain, shown in Studio. Robinhood Chain hunts run on paper for now. Where live
  trading isn't open yet, the button reads <strong>Live trading opens soon</strong>.
</Para>
<H3>Before you go live</H3>
<ul className="db-list">
  <li>The hunt must be running. Resume or renew it first if it is paused or expired.</li>
  <li>You need a wallet and a buy size for that chain, set with Dale.</li>
  <li>Watch the hunt on paper first.</li>
</ul>
<H3>How to go live</H3>
<ol className="db-list db-list--numbered">
  <li>Open the hunt and find the <strong>Real money</strong> panel.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Go live (real money)</strong>.</li>
  <li>Check the confirmation: amount per buy, slippage, wallet, the safety ratings it buys and the exit plan.</li>
  <li>Press <strong>Confirm and go live</strong>. The card turns lime and reads <strong>LIVE · real money</strong>.</li>
</ol>
<Para>
  Going live takes over the chain's real auto buy slot. If your own auto buy was on for
  that chain, going live replaces it, and it comes back when you stop live trading.
</Para>

<H2 id="safety">Safety on every live buy</H2>
<Para>
  Live hunts use the same trade safety gate as Dale. Tokens the scan marks malicious are
  never bought. On Solana, hunts handle standard tokens and safe Token 2022 tokens, and
  Token 2022 tokens with risky settings are always skipped.
</Para>

<H2 id="stop">Stop live</H2>
<Para>
  Press <strong>Stop live</strong> on the hunt page and confirm. Open positions keep their
  exits and still sell automatically. The hunt keeps running on paper.
</Para>
