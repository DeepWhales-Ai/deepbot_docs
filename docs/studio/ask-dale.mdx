<Para>
  Asking Dale inside Studio is a PRO feature. Press <strong>Ask Dale</strong> at the top,
  or <strong>Ask Dale about this hunt</strong> on a hunt page.
</Para>

<H2 id="what">What Dale can do</H2>
<ul className="db-list">
  <li>Explain filters, exits, tokens and the market.</li>
  <li>Check a draft with "Check this hunt before I save".</li>
  <li>Answer questions like "Why did this trade lose?" or "What does liquidity locked mean?"</li>
  <li>Look at your own hunts and their results, and suggest changes in the direction you want, safer or bolder.</li>
</ul>

<H2 id="limits">You apply the changes</H2>
<Para>
  Inside Studio, Dale offers advice and can't apply changes. Use the controls on your Hunts
  page to build, pause, edit or delete a hunt, and the hunt's Real money controls to go
  live or stop live. Dale never turns live trading on for you, and he can't see your
  wallets, balances or other people's accounts.
</Para>

<H2 id="telegram">Settings that live in Telegram</H2>
<Para>
  The amount per buy, slippage and daily cap are set with Dale on Telegram. Prompts marked
  <strong> Do it on Telegram</strong> open Dale there.
</Para>
