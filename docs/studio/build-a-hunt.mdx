<PageHead

  lede={<>Open Strategy Studio and press <strong>New hunt</strong>. Answer three questions.</>}
/>

| Question | What it sets |
|---|---|
| **Which chain?** | Which network the hunt watches. One hunt watches one chain. |
| **How much risk?** | Careful, balanced or aggressive. Sets the safety rating, market cap, liquidity, tax and holders filters, and a stop loss. |
| **How fast in and out?** | Minutes, hours, days or 30 days. Sets the buy window, profit targets and trailing stop. |

<Para>
  You can open <strong>Advanced</strong> to fine tune the filters (buy window, safety
  rating, market cap, liquidity, holders, taxes and more), or leave the defaults.
</Para>

<H2 id="next">What happens next</H2>
<ul className="db-list">
  <li>The hunt starts scanning immediately and keeps going 24/7.</li>
  <li>When a token matches, the hunt records a paper trade (or, on PRO with live mode on, buys it).</li>
  <li>Every open position has a stop loss and take profit, and the hunt exits for you.</li>
  <li>Your hunt card shows its results and win rate as trades close.</li>
</ul>

<H2 id="manage">Pause, edit or delete</H2>
<Para>
  Pause a hunt at any time; nothing new is bought while paused. Edit its settings, or
  delete it. Its history stays with you.
</Para>
