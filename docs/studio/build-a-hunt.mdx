<H2 id="guided">Guided mode: three questions</H2>
<H3>01 Which chain?</H3>
<Para>
  Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Solana or Robinhood Chain. Your hunt watches every new launch
  on that chain.
</Para>

<H3>02 How much risk?</H3>

| Choice | What it sets |
|---|---|
| **Careful** | Bigger tokens with more liquidity and holders. Market cap $150k to $1.5M, at least $25k liquidity, tax no more than 3%, at least 200 holders, liquidity locked (EVM chains), stop loss at 10%. |
| **Balanced** | Mid size tokens. Market cap up to $500k, at least $8k liquidity, tax no more than 6%, at least 80 holders, liquidity locked (EVM chains), stop loss at 10%. |
| **Aggressive** | Tiny fresh launches. Market cap up to $150k, at least $1k liquidity, tax no more than 10%, at least 20 holders, no liquidity lock check on Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base and Robinhood Chain, stop loss at 10%. |

<Para>
  All three block tokens the safety scan marks as malicious. On Solana there is no
  liquidity lock setting: every level applies the dev holding floor and the token program
  guard instead. On Robinhood Chain the tax and holder limits are not offered.
</Para>

<H3>03 How fast in and out?</H3>

| Choice | Buy window | Take profit | Trailing stop |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quick flips** (minutes) | First 10 minutes on Solana and Robinhood Chain, 30 on Base, 60 on Ethereum and BNB Chain | Sell 50% at a 25% gain, 50% at a 60% gain | 8% gap, from a 30% gain |
| **Steady** (hours) | First hour | Sell 25% at a 50% gain, 25% at a 100% gain, 50% at a 200% gain | 15% gap, from a 100% gain |
| **Patient** (days) | First day | Sell 33% at a 100% gain, 33% at a 300% gain, 34% at a 600% gain | 25% gap, from a 200% gain |
| **Established** (30 days) | Tokens up to 30 days old that already trade | Same as Patient | Same as Patient |

<Para>
  Press <strong>See every gate this sets</strong> to open Advanced mode with your answers
  loaded. Advanced mode always shows the exact numbers. These are settings, not expected
  results.
</Para>

<H2 id="buy-size">Buy size</H2>
<Para>
  You don't set the amount per buy in the builder. Your buy size, slippage and daily cap
  are set per chain with Dale on Telegram. Paper trades use a fixed trade size per chain.
</Para>

<H2 id="quiet">Tight settings can mean no buys for hours</H2>
<Para>
  Every gate removes tokens. A very short buy window with high liquidity, many holders and
  a high convergence score can mean nothing matches for hours, or even days. If a hunt is
  quiet, loosen one gate at a time, starting with the buy window or the convergence score.
</Para>
