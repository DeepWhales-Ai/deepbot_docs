<H2 id="share">Share a hunt</H2>
<Para>
  Turn on <strong>Share to Community</strong> on the hunt page. Tick <strong>Share as
  Anonymous</strong> to hide your name. People who clone get your filters and exit plan,
  never your wallet, trade size or results. <strong>Unshare</strong> removes it.
</Para>

<H2 id="community">Community</H2>
<Para>
  Browse shared hunts, sorted by <strong>Most cloned</strong> or <strong>Newest</strong>.
  Press <strong>Clone to my hunts</strong> to copy one. The copy starts on paper, with the
  filters and exit plan, never the owner's wallet, trade size or results.
</Para>

<H2 id="league">The League</H2>
<Para>
  Shared hunts enter the weekly <strong>League</strong>. Hunts are ranked by
  <strong> median return</strong>, the typical result across their closed trades, so one
  lucky trade can't top the table. <strong>Weekly competitions</strong> run on top of the
  League, with a fresh table every week.
</Para>

<H2 id="winners-room">The Winners Room</H2>
<Para>
  <strong>The Winners Room</strong> is a chat for PRO traders, with <strong>Top
  strategies</strong> ranked on closed paper trades. A strategy stays unranked until it has
  enough closed trades. You agree to the room terms before your first post.
</Para>

<H2 id="board">The Board</H2>
<Para>
  New tokens per chain, ranked by <strong>PULSE</strong>.
</Para>
