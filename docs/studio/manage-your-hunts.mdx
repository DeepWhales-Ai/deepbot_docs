<H2 id="running">Is my hunt running?</H2>
<Para>
  The card reads <strong>Paper running</strong>, or <strong>LIVE · real money</strong> if
  it is live. The hunt page Activity section lists the latest buys and sells. Nothing yet
  means it hasn't bought anything.
</Para>

<H2 id="edit">Editing a hunt (PRO)</H2>
<Para>
  Open the hunt and press <strong>Edit</strong>, <strong>Edit filters</strong> or
  <strong> Edit exits</strong>, then <strong>Save changes</strong>. Changes apply from the
  next token that matches. The chain can't be changed. Stop live or resume a paused hunt
  before you save. Free accounts can't edit.
</Para>

<H2 id="pause">Pause, renew and expiry (PRO)</H2>
<Para>
  <strong>Pause</strong> stops a hunt matching new tokens; open positions still follow the
  exit plan. Pause is for hunts that aren't live; a live hunt uses <strong>Stop live</strong>.
  Hunts expire a set time after they were created or last renewed. Press
  <strong> Renew</strong> to run one again. It comes back on paper, not live.
</Para>

<H2 id="delete">Deleting a hunt</H2>
<Para>
  On the hunt page, press <strong>Delete</strong>. The hunt stops, its results leave your
  hunts and a shared hunt leaves Community. There is no undo. A live hunt shows
  <strong> Stop live and delete</strong>. Deleting an active hunt frees a slot.
</Para>

<H2 id="nothing">Why has it bought nothing?</H2>
<ul className="db-list">
  <li>The filters are too tight. This is the most common reason.</li>
  <li>It is paused or expired.</li>
  <li>It says Paper starts soon.</li>
  <li>The buy window is under 10 minutes on Ethereum, BNB Chain or Base.</li>
  <li>A Solana hunt has Safe Token 2022 turned off.</li>
  <li>Unscored tokens is set to Skip while you have a convergence floor.</li>
</ul>
<Para>
  A real buy can also be refused by the safety check, a missing setup in Dale, a used up
  daily cap, or not enough balance in the buying wallet. Paper trades ignore balance and caps.
</Para>
