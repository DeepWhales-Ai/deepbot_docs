<H2 id="risk">A calm word on risk</H2>
<Para>
  Crypto is volatile, and new tokens are the most volatile of all. Paper results show how a
  plan would have done. They don't predict how it will do. Stop loss and take profit help,
  but they are not guaranteed, and a sale can happen at a worse price than expected.
</Para>
<Para>
  Nothing in Strategy Studio, or anything Dale says, is financial advice. Start small, test
  on paper first, and only trade what you can afford to lose.
</Para>
