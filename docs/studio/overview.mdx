<H2 id="hunt">What a hunt does</H2>
<Para>
  A hunt watches every new token that launches on the chain you pick. When a token passes
  all of your rules (Studio calls them <strong>filters</strong> or <strong>gates</strong>),
  the hunt buys it. It then sells on your <strong>exit plan</strong>: your take profit
  levels, your stop loss and, if you add one, a trailing stop.
</Para>

<H2 id="paper-live">Paper and live</H2>
<Para>
  <strong>Paper</strong> means pretend money. Paper trades use live market prices, so you
  can see how your hunt would have done, but nothing real is bought or sold.
</Para>
<Para>
  <strong>Live</strong> means real money. When you go live with a hunt, it buys with real
  money from your wallet every time a token passes its filters. Live buying is on PRO, on
  Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base and Solana. Robinhood Chain hunts run on paper for now.
</Para>

<H2 id="chains">Chains</H2>
<Para>
  Studio runs hunts on 5 chains: Ethereum, BNB Chain, Base, Solana and Robinhood Chain.
  One hunt watches one chain.
</Para>

<H2 id="first-visit">Your first visit</H2>
<Para>
  The first time you open Studio you see a short notice called <strong>Before you
  start</strong>. Tick <strong>I understand and accept these risks</strong> and press
  <strong> Continue</strong>. Ticking the box only records that you have read the notice.
  It does not turn anything on, make anything live or spend any money. A short guided tour
  follows on most pages, and you can replay it any time with <strong>Show me around</strong>.
</Para>

<Callout kind="info" title="Trading carries risk">
  Prices can move quickly and you could lose some or all of the money you trade. Stop loss

  predict real ones. Nothing in Strategy Studio is financial advice.
</Callout>
