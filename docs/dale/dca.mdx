<H2 id="what">Can Dale run a DCA strategy for you?</H2>
<Para>
  Yes. Sonar DCA lets you build an averaging strategy in conversation and then runs it
  for you. Describe the token and the schedule, and Dale scans the target, provisions a
  dedicated deposit address for the strategy, funds it from your Dale wallet, and
  creates it. There is no external interface to sign into and no dashboard to
  configure. You can average into a position and average out of one, so the same
  engine handles both accumulation and scheduled selling. One thing to understand up
  front: a strategy does not execute in place on your Dale wallet. Funds move to a
  dedicated address that the strategy controls for its lifetime, and proceeds settle
  back to your Dale wallet when it completes. Your destination wallet is registered at
  creation, so there is no manual withdrawal step waiting for you at the end.
</Para>

<H2 id="availability">Which chains does DCA run on?</H2>
<Para>
  Sonar DCA runs on <Lime>Ethereum, BSC, Base and Solana</Lime>. Polygon is integrated
  at the routing layer but is not active for strategies you set.
</Para>

<H2 id="settlement">Where do the proceeds go?</H2>
<Para>
  Your destination wallet is registered at strategy creation, so there is no manual
  withdrawal step. On completion, proceeds settle back to your Dale wallet,
  typically within hours, as the chain's native token.
</Para>

<H2 id="safety">What stops a DCA into a bad token?</H2>
<Para>
  Every DCA buy target is scanned before creation. Malicious, unverified and scan
  failed tokens are blocked before funds move. Sells are not gated, since exits are
  always allowed.
</Para>
<Callout kind="lime" title="Held, not abandoned">
  If a funding transfer fails or returns an ambiguous result, the position is held
  and escalated rather than silently abandoned.
</Callout>
