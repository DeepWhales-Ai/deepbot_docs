<H2 id="what">Can you trade by voice?</H2>
<Para>
  Yes. Send Dale a voice message and Dale acts on it the same way it acts on typed
  text. Say what you want to buy, how much, and on which chain, and the request goes
  through the same intent parsing, the same safety checks and the same routing as a
  typed instruction. Voice runs on every account and works in English. It is
  <Lime> inbound only</Lime>: you speak to Dale, and Dale answers in text. Dale does
  not send voice messages back. Every voice trade goes through a confirmation step
  before it executes, so nothing is signed straight off the audio. By default you
  confirm that step with a tap. If you would rather authorize by voice instead, a
  voice PIN is available and you switch it on yourself. It is off until you do.
</Para>

<H2 id="how">How do you send a voice trade?</H2>
<Para>
  Record a voice message in the chat the way you would to a person. Say the token, the
  amount and the chain. Dale transcribes it, works out the intent, runs the safety
  pipeline, and comes back with a confirmation before anything executes. If the audio
  is unclear or the request is ambiguous, Dale asks rather than guessing.
</Para>

<H2 id="pin">Is voice PIN on by default?</H2>
<Para>
  No. Voice PIN is <Lime>off by default</Lime> and stays off until you turn it on with{' '}
  <Mono>/voicepin</Mono>. Leaving it off does not remove the confirmation step. Tap PIN
  and tap confirm are always available and are what you get out of the box. Turning
  voice PIN on gives you a second way to authorize, not a replacement for the gate.
</Para>
