<H2 id="is">What is Dale?</H2>
<Para>
  Dale is the trader facing product of DeepBot, a conversational trader that runs
  inside Telegram. You ask for a swap in plain language and Dale checks the route,
  surfaces the fee, and executes. You ask it to watch a launch and it watches and pings
  you. You ask it to protect a position from a rug and it watches the mempool and exits
  ahead of the attacker. There are no commands to learn and no syntax to get right.
  Type it, or send Dale a voice note.{' '}
  <a href="/dale/voice" className="db-link">Trading by voice →</a>
  Dale trades across <Lime>six chains</Lime>: Ethereum, BSC, Base, Polygon, Solana and
  Robinhood Chain, from one wallet. The trade fee is 1% on the EVM
  chains and 1.5% on Solana. Behind every decision sits the convergence engine, which reads community
  activity across the network and weighs it against on chain router behaviour.
  Detection, not prediction. Dale surfaces what it sees and you decide when to move.
</Para>
