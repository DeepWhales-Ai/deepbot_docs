<H2 id="supported">Can Dale send tokens to another wallet?</H2>
<Para>
  Yes. Dale sends on both the EVM chains and Solana, in the same conversation you
  trade in. On <Lime>Ethereum, BSC, Base and Polygon</Lime> you can send the native
  asset or any standard token you hold. On <Lime>Solana</Lime> you can send native SOL
  or any standard SPL token. Tell Dale the token, the amount and the destination,
  confirm it, and the transaction goes out. The confirmed result, including the
  transaction hash or signature, comes back to chat within seconds. Addresses resolve
  the way you would expect, with ENS on the EVM chains and SNS on Solana, so you can
  send to a name instead of a raw address. A percentage send or an "all" send reads
  your actual on chain balance at the moment of sending rather than a cached figure,
  so it does not strand dust behind and does not fail for being a fraction over what
  you hold.
</Para>

<H2 id="precision">How precise is the amount?</H2>
<Para>
  A percentage or "all" send reads the actual raw on chain balance before
  calculating the amount. Precision beyond the token's allowed decimal places is
  rejected before broadcast, not after. Chain errors are translated into plain
  English before they reach you.
</Para>

<H2 id="token-2022">Are Token 2022 assets supported?</H2>
<Para>
  Yes. Token 2022 program assets are supported across the screener, trading and
  transfers, alongside standard SPL tokens. You do not need to know which program a
  token uses. Dale resolves it and handles the transfer semantics for you.
</Para>
