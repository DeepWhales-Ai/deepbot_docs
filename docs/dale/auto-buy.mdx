<H2 id="what">What it does</H2>
<Para>
  When a screener match arrives, Auto Buy executes the trade through Dale without
  further input. Configurable parameters are buy amount, slippage, per sweep cap
  and daily spend cap.
</Para>

<H2 id="one-token">The one token rule</H2>
<Para>
  A given token can only ever be bought once through the screener per user, no
  matter how many times it matches. This is permanent, by design.
</Para>

<H2 id="entitlements">Entitlements</H2>
<Para>
  Auto Buy is a Pro capability. Up to five armed strategies per user. Automated
  exits armed as part of an auto buy count toward the same five strategy cap.
</Para>

<H2 id="availability">Availability</H2>
<Para>
  Live on Ethereum. Base and BSC land next. Auto buy outcomes that fail or need
  attention arrive inside Dale in the same chat as every other trade result.
</Para>

<Callout kind="info" title="Auto Buy does not fire on Solana">
  Screener matches on Solana are delivered for you to buy manually. Auto Buy itself
  runs on Ethereum. Selling and protective exits do run automatically on Solana.
